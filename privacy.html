Privacy Policy

App Portfolio & Digital Projects
Last Updated: May 2026

1. Commitment to Privacy

This privacy policy applies to the mobile software applications, utilities, and digital experiences developed by Camden McClelland. Privacy is a foundational priority. These applications are built from the ground up to protect user data by ensuring transparency and minimizing data footprint.

2. Information Collection and Use

Our applications are designed to operate as localized client utilities. This means that:

  • No Personal Data Collection: We do not collect, harvest, or transmit personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, physical location, or device identifiers).
  • Local Storage Architecture: Any configuration states, game records, names list, or statistics metrics you enter inside the apps are stored strictly on your local device using standard native sandbox files or browser LocalStorage. This data never leaves your hardware.
  • No Background Tracking: Our software contains no built-in location telemetry tools or invisible usage trackers.

3. Third-Party Services

Our applications do not integrate third-party ad networks, telemetry frameworks, or diagnostic tracking software that silently monitor behavior or compile advertising profiles across your device history.

4. Children's Privacy

Because our applications strictly collect zero information, they comply entirely with children's online safety standards across all jurisdictions. We do not knowingly or unknowingly collect data from children or any other demographic group.

5. Changes to This Policy

This policy may occasionally be updated to reflect minor technical changes or platform adaptations. Any updates will be posted transparently on this webpage, along with an updated effective date modification.

6. Contact

If you have any questions or require troubleshooting assistance regarding app functionality or software privacy architectures, feel free to get in touch via the main communications channels on the home network.
